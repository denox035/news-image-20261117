One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026, for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117). The poster
names the day in a red banner — **17 NOVEMBER 2026** — and shows the day's
five most consequential stories, each with a flat illustration and a short
headline:

1. **Kerch Bridge hit again** — Ukraine struck the bridge linking Russia to
   Crimea for a second day running, collapsing a section; Russia hit Kyiv's
   Dnipro bridges in return.
2. **Tankers struck near Hormuz** — oil tankers hit mines near the Strait of
   Hormuz as a third US aircraft carrier arrived in the region.
3. **Xi, Trump and Putin to meet Thursday** — China confirmed the first
   three-way meeting of the three leaders, in Shenzhen on 19 November.
4. **Japan scrambles jets** — Tokyo protested after suspected Russian
   aircraft violated Japanese airspace.
5. **Pentagon fails audit, 9th year** — auditors again could not verify the
   US Defense Department's books.

Facts were checked against same-day reporting (Al Jazeera / Reuters via
Algenom, Just Security's Early Edition, Zark News) on 18 November 2026.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness — two runs write the same
bytes. Requires Node 22, stdlib only.
